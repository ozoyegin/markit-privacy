# Markit User Guide

## What does Markit do?

Markit is a personal learning app that lets you **save the words and expressions you encounter together with the sentence where they appear**, organise them, and review them at the right time while learning a foreign language.

The aim is not merely to collect words; it is to help you learn a word in its real context and remember it for a long time through regular review.

The basic workflow is:

1. Create a list for the language you are learning.
2. Save words or expressions with their translation and example sentence.
3. Study new records in **Learn**.
4. Study records whose review time has arrived in **Review**.
5. Follow your progress on the **Progress** screen.

> Some button and menu names in the app interface may be in English. Where necessary, this guide uses the names shown on screen.

## Creating a list

Tap the add-list button on the home screen. Give the list a name, choose the learning language, and optionally set a colour and reading voice. Save the list with **Save**.

Language selection is important so records are read aloud in the correct language. You can create separate lists for different languages, sources, or topics, and sublists for books and units.

Lists are ordered by their creation date, with the newest list shown first. Editing a list name, colour, language, or voice later does not change this order.

## Adding and editing records

A record consists of:

- The word or expression to learn
- Translation
- The source sentence containing the word

Open a list and tap **+** to add a record. An example sentence is not required, but it makes remembering a word in context easier.

When you tap a record card, the **Edit record** window opens. You can edit the record, translation, and sentence there, or delete the record after confirmation.

The same word or expression can be saved more than once. This lets you study different meanings, translations, or usage sentences separately.

## Speech controls

The speaker button beside records reads the text in the language of the relevant list.

- Tap: starts reading using the displayed repeat and speed values.
- Tap again while reading: stops reading.
- Swipe up on the speaker: changes the repeat count.
- Swipe down on the speaker: changes the current reading speed.

**Repeat** above the speaker shows the repeat count; **Speed** below it shows the reading speed. These settings apply only to the record currently shown and do not change the list's permanent voice setting.

## Learn, Review, and Game

### Learn

Studies new records or records at the beginning of their learning process in small groups and with different exercises.

### Review

Shows records whose review time has arrived. After successful study, a record's level increases and its next review is scheduled further in the future.

### Game

Lets you practise records through **Flashcards**, **Guess**, **Match**, and **Write** exercises. Game supports the Learn and Review routine; it does not replace due Review sessions.

## Levels and the review system

New records start at level `L0` and progress up to `L5` through study sessions. Each record's level and next review date are stored separately.

- Review intervals are shorter at lower levels.
- Review intervals become longer as the level increases.
- `L5` records are also shown in Review when their time arrives.
- An unsuccessful record can remain at the same level and be reviewed sooner.

List cards and the **Progress** screen provide summary information about study time, correct and incorrect answers, levels, and progress for each list.

## Adding records with Miray Browser

Miray Browser lets you add words and expressions directly to Markit while reading a web page.

1. Select the target record list.
2. Select a word or expression on the web page.
3. Tap **Add to Markit**.
4. Check the record, translation, and source sentence.
5. Save with **Save record**.

Saved expressions are highlighted using the colour of their list. Tap a highlighted record to see its translation, hear it, or edit it with **Edit**. If the same expression appears in more than one record, Markit shows the relevant records separately.

You can change the target list without leaving the page by tapping **Markit saves to**.

## Tabs and Bookmarks

The tab-count button in the address bar shows open pages. From there, you can move between tabs, open a new tab, and close a tab with the **x** button or by swiping it aside.

Each tab keeps its page, target record list, and Reader position separately. **Bookmarks** in the three-dot menu saves pages you want to open again later.

## Reading aloud with Page Reader

The movable speaker button on a page opens Page Reader controls. On suitable web pages, Reader reads the text sentence by sentence and highlights the sentence being read.

- **Play / Pause:** starts or pauses reading.
- **Stop:** ends reading.
- **Previous / Next:** moves to the previous or next sentence.
- **Repeat:** repeats the current sentence.
- **Tap to start:** lets you choose the sentence where reading starts.
- **Volume:** changes the volume.
- **Speed:** sets reading speed between `0.25x-3.00x`.
- **Settings:** changes the reading language, voice, and highlight colour.

Reader keeps the last reading position separately for every open page. When you select the same page again from open tabs, the sentence where you stopped is prepared; tap **Play** to continue.

For Premium users, speech can continue when the screen turns off or the phone is locked. In Free use, active speech stops when the screen turns off.

## Reminder and daily goal

Use **Review reminder** in the profile menu to set a daily notification time for records whose review time has arrived. Android notification permission must be enabled for the reminder to work.

The daily goal on the home screen helps you track the studying you have done that day. When the goal is completed, a celebration animation appears in the Today area.

## Backup and transfer

Markit data is kept in the local database on your device. Create a current backup before uninstalling the app or changing devices.

- **Backup:** saves lists, records, learning levels, review dates, progress, and supported Browser data.
- **Restore:** restores a complete Markit backup.
- **Export list:** exports the selected list.
- **Import list:** imports a list file. You can keep learning progress or restart records from level `L0`.

You can keep the backup file on the device or in a trusted cloud folder. A Premium subscription being linked to a Google Play account does not automatically back up local learning data.

## Free and Premium

The Free version provides basic list, record, Learn, Review, Game, and Browser use. Some usage limits and ad intervals may vary according to the current configuration.

Premium generally:

- Removes ads.
- Removes list, record, and bulk-operation limits.
- Unlocks advanced Reader use.
- Removes Backup and Export limits.
- Allows reading aloud to continue when the screen is off or the phone is locked.

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. An active subscription can be verified again with the same Google Play account; local learning data must also be backed up separately.

## Short usage tips

- Save a word with its source sentence whenever possible.
- Check the target list before saving in Browser.
- Study new records in small Learn sessions.
- Complete Review records regularly without letting them accumulate.
- Add pages you want to return to later to Bookmarks.
- Back up regularly and keep the backup in a second location.
